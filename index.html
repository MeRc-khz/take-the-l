🚂 TAKE THE 'L'

The inbound train runs flat across the yard, slips behind the tunnel to drop one train height, and comes back the other way. Each level sends a longer train with more engines to catch.

Tap the engines on the train to save them. Tap a car to grab it, then tap a track to load it.

Cars arrive in colour batches of 2–5. A track only takes cars once it has an engine, and only one colour at a time — stack as long as you like.

At 3 cars or more, buy a caboose to close the train and move it off the track so you can start another one. The longer the train, the more it pays.

Every train you send out also backs the inbound train up one switchback level — up to one level per track in the yard.

Tapped a track with only 1–2 cars and it shunts them back onto the back of the inbound train — −6 a car, and they have to ride the whole hill again.

🃏 Wild cards join any colour already on a track. Buy one for 30, or grab one off the main train when it rides past.

Everything costs points — stack long, but don't strand a train.
