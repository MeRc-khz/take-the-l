🚂 TAKE THE 'L'

The inbound train zig-zags down from the top of the yard. Each level sends a longer train with more engines to catch.

Tap the engines on the train to save them. Tap a car to grab it, then tap a track to load it.

Cars arrive in colour groups of 2–5. A track only takes cars once it has an engine, and only one colour at a time — fill the whole group to ship it and empty the track.

Out of engines? Buy one with your points.
